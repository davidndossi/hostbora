About HostBora

HostBora is built by Artbel Technologies, a Tanzanian software company based in Dar es Salaam. We design tools for landlords, property managers, and hosts who operate in Tanzania and across East Africa — with local currency, offline use, and real-world workflows in mind.

HostBora helps hosts and landlords run short-stay stays and long-term rentals from one app. Switch between the BnB and Rent workspaces any time.

BnB workspace

Manage listings and units, track bookings and today's check-ins, use your host calendar, sync external calendars, record guest payments, and view reports.

Rent workspace

Track tenants and leases, manage rent payments and reminders, monitor expenses and monthly performance, handle staff, and review smart utility usage where set up.

Shared across both

Add properties and expenses, message guests or tenants, keep documents in your property vault, and continue with secure on-device data when you are offline.
